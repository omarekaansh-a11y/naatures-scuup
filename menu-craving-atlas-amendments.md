# Craving Atlas Review Amendments

The menu must now be treated as a paced editorial sequence rather than a visually refined catalogue. Each chapter needs a stronger opening lead moment, a visible course index, and more decisive type contrast before compact browsing resumes. The top category controls will become a numbered menu index with a small route-line connection, avoiding generic filter-pill language.

Photography will retain its unique category relevance but will be graded by appetite cue: fennel freshness for soups, salads, South Indian and accompaniments; deeper savoury maroon for starters, mains, breads, Chinese, noodles, rolls and snacks; mango warmth for bakery; and rose/mango playfulness for drinks and ice creams. Category titles will remain fully opaque cream against these veils.

Dish records will stay compact, but the first record of each category will take on a clearer editorial lead role. Cormorant will give the lead name its visual authority; Manrope will keep descriptions, category metadata, price, and display status disciplined and secondary. The scoop stamp and craving ribbon will recur at the navigation/index and chapter thresholds as one coherent grammar.
