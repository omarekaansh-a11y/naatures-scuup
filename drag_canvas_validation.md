# Tactile Food Canvas Validation

The new “Pull the table closer” section was reviewed in the full desktop journey and the mobile site. It reads as a substantial maroon editorial chapter rather than a carousel, uses the seven owner-supplied food images once each, and contains no foreground artificial food imagery.

The interaction uses direct pointer and touch input, constrained transform-based movement, gentle release momentum, horizontal-drag gating to preserve vertical touch scrolling, and keyboard controls for left, right, home, and end. The mobile instruction remains available until a visitor successfully drags the canvas.
