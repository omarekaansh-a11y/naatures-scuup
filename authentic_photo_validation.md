# Authentic Photography Validation

The Naatures Scuup website now uses owner-supplied restaurant photographs for all foreground food moments: story, dessert chapter, pasta feature, and the food gallery. The original generated hero remains solely as the user-approved background treatment.

Desktop and mobile reviews confirmed the authentic photos have intentional warm crops and fit the established editorial sequence. The logo framing was reduced to a small authenticity mark, allowing the Mall Road Maroon, warm rice-paper palette, and typography to remain dominant.
