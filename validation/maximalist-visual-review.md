## Unified Menu Repair

The Full Menu now renders every chapter as one complete, photo-led panel. The actual food image fills the panel without an image gradient, filter, or separate visual cell. Headings and supporting copy sit in opaque cream and lime printed labels with dark ink, giving contrast without blending into the photo. The previously busy transition geometry is now one broad wave with a dotted stitched seam. Desktop and 390px phone captures preserve the full-panel treatment and do not show detached image, gradient, and heading blocks.
