# Category Visual Audit

## Findings

The first category plate set had a coherent maroon treatment but several food images did not clearly substantiate their headings. Starters read as a grain bowl, Main Course as a salad, Breads as samosas, Rice & Biryani as a person at a table, Chinese as rice, Snacks as pasta, Rolls as a noodle bowl, Maggi as a burger, Accompaniments as pizza, Drinks as coffee, and Bakery Specials as cake. These mismatches weakened the Full Menu’s factual display role and made several outlined headings blend too closely with their backdrops.

## Replacement Direction

The updated set uses only distinct, real food photographs chosen for the relevant category: samosas for Starters; paneer curry for Main Course; paneer and naan for Breads; vegetable biryani for Rice & Biryani; dosa for South Indian; vegetable Hakka noodles for Chinese and for Fried Rice & Noodles; vada pav for Burgers & Sandwiches; a street-food spread for Snacks; paneer kathi rolls for Rolls; Maggi noodles for Maggi; raita and papad for Accompaniments; chilled dessert mocktails for Drinks; and paneer puffs for Bakery Specials. The existing soup/salad image and the uniquely reserved restaurant images for Pizza & Pasta and Desserts & Ice Creams remain appropriate.

## Contrast Direction

Every category title uses an opaque, high-contrast cream or ink treatment rather than the former transparent outlined display. A stronger left-side veil carries title and supporting copy, while category-specific mango, fennel, rose, and cream accents distinguish savoury, vegetarian-fresh, chilled, and bakery chapters without reducing legibility.

## Higgsfield Decision

Higgsfield remains enabled but its image generation requires a Basic plan or higher in the current workspace. The replacement set therefore uses high-quality real photographic sources and original layout/colour work; no generated foreground food is introduced.

## Live Interaction Validation

The rendered Full Menu now exposes every category as a numbered index entry—from `00 All Items` through `17 Bakery Specials`—with the required ampersands visible in the relevant entries. The index retained direct selection behavior after its editorial restyle. In the live page content, the Starters chapter visibly uses its samosa image, high-contrast chapter treatment, and the `01 The first plate` lead record before compact dish browsing resumes.
