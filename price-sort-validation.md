# Price Sort Validation

## Verified on 20 August 2026

The Full Menu lists both requested sort options: **Price: low to high** and **Price: high to low**. The low-to-high All Items view correctly reorders priced entries within each displayed chapter, for example: Starters begins at Chinese Bhel ₹180 and rises through Paneer Tikka ₹280; Soups & Salads begins at Onion Salad ₹60 and rises through Mix Fruit Salad ₹120; Main Course begins at Jeera Aloo ₹140.

Prices are displayed only for the 186 exact-name public Magicpin matches. The remaining 18 dishes have no displayed price and are intentionally placed after verified priced dishes in either price-sort mode rather than being assigned an inferred value.
