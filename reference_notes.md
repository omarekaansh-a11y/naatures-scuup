# Reference Study — Madie (High-Level Principles Only)

The public Madie website uses a restrained editorial restaurant experience: a full-viewport food-led opening, small utility navigation, oversized type with strong line breaks, carefully staged white space, richly coloured location information, and a curated menu interaction. Its central lesson is not a layout to duplicate, but a **design discipline**: make one food story feel tactile, distinctive, and easy to navigate.

For Naatures Scuup, the original design will retain only broadly applicable principles: immersive food imagery, a small persistent navigation, expressive scale contrast between display and supporting type, a sequence of editorial sections, subtle scroll-state transitions, and a location/ordering conclusion. It will use its own Kanpur-focused palette, copy, imagery, layout geometry, visual motifs, and interaction details.

## Non-negotiable originality rules

- Do not copy Madie’s logo, words, imagery, illustrations, route structure, exact geometry, or distinctive graphics.
- Do not make the site appear to be an affiliated or translated version of Madie.
- Establish Naatures Scuup as a multi-cuisine vegetarian restaurant and dessert destination on Mall Road, Kanpur.
- Use the owner-supplied official logo only if it is available as an uploaded asset. No replacement mark will be created.
