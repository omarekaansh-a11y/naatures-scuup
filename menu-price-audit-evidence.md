# Public Menu Price Audit Evidence

On 27 August 2026, the public Zomato page for Naatures Scuup, Birhana Road exposed the food menu as nine image-based pages and beverages as one image-based page. The rendered page did not provide accessible, machine-readable dish-price pairs for the currently unmatched menu dishes. The existing price map therefore remains the source of directly transcribed, unambiguous item-price pairs; missing prices need image-level verification before inclusion.

The page also displayed a separate current-hours statement that conflicts with the owner-provided site schedule. It is not used for the website schedule update.

Source inspected: https://www.zomato.com/kanpur/naatures-scuup-birhana-road/menu

## Directly Readable Menu-Sheet Evidence

The first food-menu sheet shows the following unambiguous single prices: Veg Chaap ₹200, in addition to prices already mapped for Snacks and Maggi. It shows two-column prices for Cream of Tomato Soup (₹70 / ₹100), Hot & Sour Soup (₹80 / ₹100), Manchow Soup (₹80 / ₹120), Veg Corn Soup (₹70 / ₹110), and Clear Soup (₹50 / ₹80); these should be displayed as an exact price range rather than reduced to a single inferred value.

The second food-menu sheet confirms most existing Main Course values and indicates that Veg Jaipuri is ₹210, replacing the former ₹190 mapping.

The third and fourth sheets show a separate red menu design with several conflicting values, including Veg Chaap at ₹220 rather than ₹200, soups at higher two-size prices, and different prices for Chinese and pasta dishes. Because the public sheets conflict and expose no dates, they cannot be treated as a single current price source without risking inaccurate price changes. Their clearly visible additions, such as Brownie Sizzler and the older red-menu price variants, require confirmation against the current owner menu before appearing as current website prices.

The fifth sheet lists Khasta Roti ₹60, Tawa Roti ₹30, and Tawa Butter Roti ₹40, which are not present in the website catalogue. The sixth sheet visibly lists additional ice-cream flavours, including Strawberry Pulp ₹70, Banana Bonanza ₹70, Sapota/Chikoo ₹70, Blue Bubblegum ₹80, Strawberry Cheese ₹90, Black Grapes ₹70, and Mint ₹80. It also presents current conflicts for Anjeer Badam and Kesar Pista, shown at ₹80 versus ₹60 on the website. These additions and conflicts need owner confirmation before a public price replacement, because Zomato exposes multiple undated menu sheets with contradictory values.

The seventh sheet confirms existing values for sandwiches, pasta, pizza, Chinese dishes, and fries. The eighth sheet lists on-request cakes, pastries, and Swiss rolls, plus Extra Cheese Dip ₹40 per bowl and Extra Cheese for Pizza ₹60. These catalogue additions are readable, but the sheet does not supply individual cake, pastry, or Swiss-roll prices.

The final food-menu sheet lists Live Icecreams: Oreo ₹150, Kit Kat ₹170, Brownie ₹170, Choco Lava ₹180, Strawberry ₹160, Mango ₹160, and Banana ₹160. These dishes do not appear in the website catalogue and can be added as source-identified menu items with the listed prices.

The public beverage sheet confirms existing entries and adds readable product-price pairs that do not appear in the website catalogue: Toblerene Shake ₹250, M & M Shake ₹260, Canelle ₹170, Poppins ₹210, and Grenadine Mix ₹170. It also shows the existing drinks and shakes with the same values already mapped for the matching names. The sheet is partly cropped in the viewer, so only directly readable names and values are included.
